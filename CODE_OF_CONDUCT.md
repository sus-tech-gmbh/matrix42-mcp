# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. Most people here are debugging a production system on a bad day.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Assume good faith. A confusing report usually means someone is stuck, not careless.
- Keep criticism about the code, the request or the reasoning — never the person.
- Accept that Matrix42 deployments differ wildly. "It works on mine" is not a rebuttal.
- Respect a maintainer's "no". This is volunteer-maintained, and scope has to stay finite.

## What we will not accept

Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language or imagery, publishing
someone's private information, and any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in
a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at
[S&S Technologies GmbH](https://sus-tech.com/en), or privately to a maintainer on GitHub. Reports
are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that
do not follow this code, and may ban a contributor temporarily or permanently for behaviour they
consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
